Storytelling Research communication Multimedia

This work focuses on translating dense academic ideas into storytelling formats that are more legible, memorable, and emotionally resonant. The goal is not to simplify the research into cliché, but to make the underlying insight feel vivid and accessible to an audience beyond the specialist context.

Whether I am turning a concept into a script, visual explanation, or presentation asset, the same principle guides the process: clarity should feel like a creative decision, not a compromise.
