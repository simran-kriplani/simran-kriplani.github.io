Meat Your Match

Academic poster

Documentary Travel Visual storytelling

This short documentary explored the emotional and physical movement between urban life and a sense of refuge. I wanted the film to feel reflective and atmospheric, using landscape, rhythm, and silence to carry the story rather than relying on exposition alone.

The project was shaped by questions of transition, belonging, and memory. It became a way of translating place into feeling, turning a simple journey into a visual meditation on what it means to move toward comfort, rest, or renewal.
