**Market Research Study**: An examination of how consumers perceive and adopt emerging food technologies. This collaborative research explores the intersection of innovation, consumer psychology, and market acceptance.

Research focuses on:
- Innovation adoption curves and consumer willingness to try novel products
- Perception of technological disruption in traditional markets
- Sustainability messaging and consumer values
- Barriers and enablers of market acceptance for disruptive technologies
