**Market Research Perspective**: A comprehensive analysis of how behavioral and neurological factors influence decision-making around emerging markets and novel products. This research examines the psychological mechanisms underlying consumer adoption patterns, risk assessment, and market perception.

Drawing parallels to financial markets, this study explores:
- How cognitive biases shape perception of novel substances and emerging markets
- Risk perception and tolerance in unfamiliar domains
- Decision-making under uncertainty and novel information
- Market trends and adoption curves from a behavioral perspective
